# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically constructive open-source project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Provide reproducible technical information when reporting bugs.
- Respect privacy and never publish credentials or personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, credential theft, malicious disruption, and deliberate abuse of project infrastructure are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and project.
